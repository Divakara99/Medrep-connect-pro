Privacy Policy — MedRep Pro / MedRep Connect Pro

Last updated: 15 October 2026 · Applies to the MedRep Pro Android app (in.medrepconnect.app) and the MedRep Connect Pro website (medrepjob.netlify.app), together "MedRep".

Who we are

MedRep is a job-listing service for medical representatives and other field roles in India, created by M Divakar Reddy (Business Development Manager). Questions about this policy: use the support option inside the app, or the contact email shown in the app's Account screen.

What we collect and why

  • Account: name, email address, optional mobile number — to create and secure your account, enforce one-account-per-mobile, and show your subscription status.
  • Profile & resume: profile details (education, experience) and any resume you upload — stored privately, visible only to you, auto-deleted after 30 days, and attached by you when you apply.
  • Job activity: jobs you view, your applications history, and company-name unlocks you earn — to show your history and keep unlocked jobs unlocked.
  • Support: messages you send via AI Help / support requests, so we can reply.

Advertising (Android app only)

The Android app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. The website shows no ads.

  • AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, app-interaction and diagnostic data, and approximate (IP-based) location to serve, measure and improve ads, as described in Google's partner-sites notice.
  • Where Google's consent tools (UMP) require it, we ask for your consent before personalised ads, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • When you watch a video to unlock a job's company name, Google's servers confirm completion to us (server-side verification). We store only which job you unlocked — never your ad-viewing history.

Payments

  • Android app: subscriptions are one-time passes sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment; we never see or store card or bank details. We receive a purchase token, store only a cryptographic hash of it, and use Google's servers to verify the purchase, grant your access days, and handle refunds (a refunded pass may have its remaining granted days withdrawn).
  • Website: payments are processed by Razorpay; the same rule applies — we never see or store card/bank details and activate access only after Razorpay confirms capture to our server.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • Free accounts never receive employers' private contact details; subscribed-only fields are withheld by our server, not merely hidden on screen.
  • We do not ask for bank PINs, OTPs or passwords — in the app, by email, or by phone. Anyone asking is not us.

Sharing

We share data only with the processors needed to run MedRep: Supabase (account/database hosting), Google (AdMob ads, Google Play Billing), Razorpay (website payments) and Netlify (website hosting). When you apply for a job, the application email/WhatsApp message you confirm is sent by your own mail/messaging app to the hiring manager, with the details you reviewed.

Retention & deletion

Resumes are deleted automatically after 30 days. You may request account and data deletion at any time via in-app support; account deletion removes your account record, profile, resume, unlocks and application history. Payment records are retained as required for accounting and fraud prevention.

Children

MedRep is a jobs service intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be announced in the app and on this page with a new "last updated" date.

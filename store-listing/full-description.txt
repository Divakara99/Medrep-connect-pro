MedRep Pro — India's pharma-job network, built for medical representatives.

Find MR vacancies pinned first, plus ABM and multi-industry roles (FMCG, finance, banking, IT, healthcare, insurance, part-time and field sales) across India.

FREE, FOREVER:
• Every job's role, city, HQ, division, salary, experience and point-wise description
• Search and filters by city, HQ, category and experience
• Watch a short video on any locked job to reveal that company's name for 10 minutes — watch again any time to reopen it, with no daily limit

WITH A PLAN (one-time Google Play passes, from 1 day to 1 year, starting at ₹19):
• Company name, manager name, manager email and both contact numbers for every job
• One-tap Apply — email or WhatsApp to the hiring manager with your profile ready
• New passes add their days to your current expiry; nothing auto-renews

HOW APPLYING WORKS:
Complete your profile once (education, experience, resume) — it is saved to your account, so it follows you to any phone. Then open a job, tap Apply and confirm. Your mail app or WhatsApp opens with the application ready to send — you always review before sending.

HOW TO USE THE APP:
Tap "How to use the app" on the sign-in screen and pick your language — English, हिन्दी, తెలుగు, தமிழ், ಕನ್ನಡ, മലയാളം or मराठी. A short narrated tour plays with voice and explains unlocking a company name, the passes and applying.

PRIVACY & ADS:
The app is supported by ads (Google AdMob): a small banner on job screens, and the optional unlock videos described above. Subscribers see the banner only — never unlock videos. We never sell personal data, and employers' contact details are released by our server only to active subscribers. See the privacy policy in the app listing for full details.

Made in India for pharma field teams. New vacancies are added by our team as employers post them, and priority (pinned) roles are marked so urgent openings are easy to spot.
